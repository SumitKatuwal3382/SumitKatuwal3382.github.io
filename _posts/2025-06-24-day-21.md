what_i_learned: |
 Today, I learned how to use the Football API to fetch fixtures, match statistics, and event data for a specific team and season. I built a Python script that collects and organizes match data, including team performance and key moments from each game. I integrated OpenAI’s GPT-4o model to automatically generate professional summaries based on the data retrieved. This involved constructing a detailed prompt that includes team stats and events to produce accurate match reports. I wrote 25 match stats and fixture details, summarized each one, and I am now using those 25 stats to train our model.



blockers: |
  I have had blockers too, especially when dealing with inconsistent or missing data from the API, which sometimes caused errors in fetching statistics or events. Another challenge was fine-tuning the prompt to ensure the AI generated summaries that were both accurate and engaging.
  
reflection: |
 Working on this project helped me understand the practical use of APIs in gathering real-time sports data and how to process it programmatically. It was a great experience combining data retrieval with AI-generated content to produce meaningful match summaries. I realized the importance of structuring prompts carefully to get accurate and engaging outputs from language models. Writing and summarizing 25 matches gave me a deeper appreciation for the details that go into sports journalism and analysis. Using this data to train our own model feels like a strong step forward in creating smarter, context-aware football insights.
